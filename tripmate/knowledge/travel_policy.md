# Acme Corp – Domestic Business Travel & Expense Policy

Version 3.2 · Effective 1 April 2026 · Owner: Finance & Travel Desk (travel@acme.example)

This policy applies to all Acme Corp employees travelling within India on company business. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹) and include taxes.

## 1. Approval before travel

1.1 Every business trip needs a Travel Request (TR) approved by the employee's manager **before** anything is booked.

1.2 Submit the TR at least **7 days** before departure. Urgent client visits may be submitted **3 days** before departure; state the reason in the business purpose.

1.3 If the total estimated cost is **above ₹75,000**, the Department Head must also approve the TR.

1.4 Trips longer than 30 nights are temporary assignments. They need HR approval instead of a TR.

## 2. City tiers

Hotel, allowance and local transport rates depend on the destination city's tier:

- **Tier 1:** Mumbai, Delhi (including Gurugram and Noida), Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune.
- **Tier 2:** Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Chandigarh, Lucknow, Indore, Coimbatore, Nagpur, Bhubaneswar, Visakhapatnam.
- **Tier 3:** every other city in India.

## 3. Employee levels

- **L1:** Associates, Engineers and Analysts.
- **L2:** Managers and Senior Specialists.
- **L3:** Directors and above.

## 4. Hotel limits (per night, including taxes)

| Level | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| L1    | ₹6,000  | ₹4,500  | ₹3,000 |
| L2    | ₹9,000  | ₹6,500  | ₹4,500 |
| L3    | ₹14,000 | ₹10,000 | ₹7,000 |

4.1 Book through the company travel portal, or a hotel on the preferred list where one is available.

4.2 If you stay above the limit, the difference is not reimbursed unless the Department Head approves it in writing before the trip, for example during a city-wide event when no hotel within the limit is available.

4.3 Staying with friends or family is allowed. No hotel amount is paid in that case, but the daily allowance still applies.

## 5. Daily allowance (meals and incidentals)

| Level | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| L1    | ₹1,500 | ₹1,200 | ₹900   |
| L2    | ₹2,000 | ₹1,600 | ₹1,200 |
| L3    | ₹3,000 | ₹2,400 | ₹1,800 |

5.1 The allowance is paid for every travel day, counting both the departure and return days (number of nights + 1).

5.2 No receipts are needed for the daily allowance.

5.3 The allowance is not reduced when a client or the company provides a meal.

## 6. Local transport

6.1 Cabs, autos and metro rides at the destination are reimbursed at actual cost, up to ₹1,500 per day in Tier 1 cities, ₹1,000 per day in Tier 2 cities and ₹700 per day in Tier 3 cities.

6.2 Airport and station transfers count toward the daily local transport limit.

## 7. Getting there

7.1 **Flights:** economy class for all levels. L3 employees may book premium economy for flights longer than 3 hours. Book at least 14 days ahead when possible. The travel desk may reject bookings that cost more than 1.5 times the cheapest available fare.

7.2 **Trains:** AC 2-tier for L1; AC first class for L2 and L3.

7.3 **Buses:** AC sleeper or AC seater for all levels.

7.4 For planning, the travel desk uses these round-trip estimates: flight ₹12,000, train ₹4,000, bus ₹2,000. Actual fares are reimbursed at cost.

## 8. Not reimbursable

- Alcohol, minibar items, and room service beyond the daily allowance
- Laundry on trips shorter than 4 nights
- Upgrades to a higher travel class, hotel room upgrades, and loyalty-program fees
- Personal entertainment, spa and gym charges
- Traffic fines, parking tickets and other penalties
- Any expenses for family members or friends travelling with you

## 9. Claims and receipts

9.1 Submit the expense report within **15 days** of returning.

9.2 Attach a receipt for every expense above ₹500, except the daily allowance.

9.3 If a receipt is lost, you may claim up to ₹2,000 per trip with a signed self-declaration.

## 10. Cancellations and changes

10.1 Cancel or change bookings as soon as plans change. Cancellation fees are reimbursed when the change was caused by business needs.

10.2 Fees caused by personal reasons are not reimbursed.

## 11. Weekend stays and personal days

11.1 A weekend stay is allowed if it lowers the total trip cost or the client requires it.

11.2 Personal days added to a business trip are at the employee's own cost. The company pays only the fare it would have paid for the business dates.

## 12. Contact

For anything this policy doesn't cover, email the Travel Desk at travel@acme.example.
